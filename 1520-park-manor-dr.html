Property Overview

A Livable Value-Add Home With Multiple Exit Strategies

This 1959-built concrete-block single-family residence sits on an unusually large 10,503 sqft (≈0.24 acre) lot in Orlando's Union Park corridor. Orange County property records independently confirm the 3-bed/2-bath configuration, 1,387 sqft living area, concrete-block construction and 1959 build date — this is a functional, move-in-viable home rather than a heavy-rehab project, with a clear path to a permitted 4th (or 5th) bedroom and a large-lot outdoor entertainment build-out.

Condition Summary

Photos and county records show a home that is substantially functional: hardwood flooring remains through much of the living and bedroom areas, Spanish-style tile runs through the kitchen and dining spaces, existing cabinetry and kitchen appliances are in place, and both bathrooms, HVAC and laundry equipment are functional. One additional living-area room presents as an unfinished cosmetic space and is a clear candidate for reconfiguration into a permitted bedroom.

Buyer to independently verify occupancy status, permitting requirements for any bedroom or garage conversion, and all condition details prior to close.

Short-Term Rental / Zoning Note

The parcel is zoned Orange County R-1, and short-term/transient rentals are generally limited to specified commercial, industrial or PD districts rather than R-1. Do not underwrite Airbnb-style short-term rental income against this property until zoning is confirmed or resolved with Orange County. Long-term rental, individually leased rooms, and furnished medium-term housing are the safer underwriting strategies, subject to confirming lease duration and occupancy structure with the county.
