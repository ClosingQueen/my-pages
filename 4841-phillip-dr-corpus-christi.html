Two Structures, One Off-Market Opportunity

A single-family main house paired with a permitted, two-story accessory dwelling unit on a fully fenced Southside Corpus Christi lot.

Main House + ADU, Separately Metered

The main house was built in 1952 and offers 3 beds / 1 bath across roughly 1,600 sqft on a single story. The ADU, added around 1985, delivers a 3 bed / 1 bath upstairs suite with its own full kitchen and exterior access — permits were pulled on the addition. Combined livable area runs approximately 2,117 sqft on a 6,055 sqft fenced lot with a carport and existing handicap ramp. Both structures run on a master water meter with separate electrical panels, which keeps a future two-tenant setup clean from a utility-billing standpoint.

The seller will clean out and fully vacate the property, and needs approximately two weeks after contract to do so. Once vacant, the property will benefit from a cosmetic refresh — paint, flooring, and kitchen and bath updates — to reach stabilized rental condition. This is a straightforward value-add play with a clear, motivated-seller timeline.
